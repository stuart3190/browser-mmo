# Amber Coast: Tidewatch

A level-five continuation of Greenvale's keeper investigation. Complete “A Promise Kept”
(`quest.greenvale.marches_home`), then speak to Elder Maren for “Letters Across the Water”.
Follow the north road into the Marches; Willow Quay's boat leads to Tidecross Harbour. Warden
Iona waits just up the shore path. The World panel and quest compass identify these travel links.

1. Deliver Maren's records to Iona. The inland bell and coastal tide gauges share the same oath.
2. Read the storm-line gauge and broken windlass, then clear two Glassback Crabs from the wreck.
   Crabs pursue slowly and have short melee reach. Nova creates space; weapon strikes finish them.
3. Read the drowned beacon oath and silence the Drowned Bellkeeper. Its amber cone freezes its
   direction for a 1.8-second windup. Move behind it or outside the marked area. Iron Guard reduces
   damage if caught. The boss takes spell damage but cannot be rooted. Return to Iona for the
   Tideglass Seal, a bound ring with stamina and both primary class attributes.

The chapter uses personal exploration and existing eligible party kill credit. Reading a mark
before accepting its quest does not complete that objective; return to survey it on the quest.
Final quest rewards are exactly once, including concurrent turn-in requests. The coastal merchant
sells Greenvale Remedies and repairs equipped gear. No daily requirement or repeatable currency
exploit is added.

The shoreline is walkable shallows. Rocks, trees and wreck ribs have shared visible/collision
footprints; survey rings and foam are decoration. Existing other atlas regions remain mostly
catalogued greybox content. This chapter does not claim those regions are finished.

After The Bell Below, Iona opens two fieldcraft commissions: Tidewatch Blade (Warrior) and Tideglass
Staff (Mage), both level five with 100 condition. Two Field Hides, one Iron Shard and 100 copper
start a 15-second craft. A solo chapter earns at least these materials from its required kills;
party companions share the existing loot-owner rules and can trade their salvage. Collect in
Skills, including after logout, then equip from Bag. Both weapons bind on equip. Recipe cards
show carried costs, class requirements and the base stat roll; bonus attributes roll on creation.
The existing paid repair service restores their condition after use.
